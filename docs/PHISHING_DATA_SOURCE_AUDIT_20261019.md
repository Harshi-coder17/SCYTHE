# Phishing dataset source audit — 2026-10-19

This audit identifies datasets that could help development or release qualification for SCYTHE's paired URL, screenshot, and scan-signal models. Dataset availability, labeling, and access terms were checked against the linked source pages on 2026-10-19. A repository's software license is not assumed to license a separate dataset.

## Strongest leads

- **PhiShark2026 — request approval before use.** The [dataset site](https://phishark2026dataset.com/) describes 53,591 paired scan records from April–July 2026 (25,598 phishing, 27,993 benign), with HTML, screenshots, redirects, DNS/RDAP, TLS, ports, and other raw evidence. Its [access terms](https://phishark2026dataset.com/access-terms/) restrict access to approved academic/nonprofit noncommercial research, require manual approval and a signed agreement, and prohibit redistribution. The [paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.23199) instead reports 67,502 observations (33,387 phishing and 34,115 screened benign). Resolve the release/count mismatch with the dataset owner before importing the 23.97 GB archive. Feed-based phish labels and popularity/reference-based benign labels are development supervision, not independent release labels; the corpus also may not include SCYTHE's exact sandbox observations.
- **Phish360 — possible visual development data.** The [project page](https://web.cs.hacettepe.edu.tr/~selman/phish360-dataset/) and [paper](https://www.mdpi.com/2076-3417/16/2/751) describe URL, HTML, and standardized screenshot samples, with phishing sourced from feeds and benign pages from popular-site lists. The archived artifacts are historical and do not contain contemporaneous SCYTHE signals. The sources describe academic/research use, but no explicit dataset license was found in this audit; do not redistribute the archive or treat it as an untouched holdout. Keep the existing Phish360 URL/domain exclusions.

## Not suitable for qualification without more terms or evidence

- **Zenodo Phishing and Legitimate Websites Dataset (record 21379702).** Its [record](https://zenodo.org/records/21379702) describes 60,000 URLs (31,641 PhishTank and 28,359 Tranco), 21,311 screenshots, and an approximately 1.8 GB archive. The record has no clear license field, describes no HTML, and uses feed/rank labels. Ask the authors to clarify rights and sample-level capture metadata before use.
- **LNU-Phish.** Its [project page](https://lnu-phish.github.io/) describes URL/HTML/screenshot and DNS evidence, but the data is from 2019, the full screenshot archive is about 34 GB, and this audit found no explicit dataset license. Its age and provenance make it a poor fit for current deployment qualification.
- **Phishpedia / PhishIntention.** The [Phishpedia repository](https://github.com/lindsey98/Phishpedia) advertises a 30K phishing benchmark with URLs, HTML, screenshots, and brands. The repository's CC0 code license does not by itself establish rights to the linked corpus. These older phishing-only artifacts could at most support experimental visual training after dataset rights and sample quality are verified; they cannot provide a benign cohort or independent holdout.
- **Hugging Face `seirin16/Dataset_Phising`.** Its [dataset page](https://huggingface.co/datasets/seirin16/Dataset_Phising) claims 1,000 manually verified URL/HTML/screenshot examples and CC-BY-NC terms, but capture dates are missing and the dataset viewer reports a schema mismatch. At most, consider it for a small noncommercial experiment after local schema and sample verification.

## Decision

No examined external corpus can currently replace both a reviewed, grouped development cohort and a later untouched holdout with independently supported sample-level labels. Proceed with PhiShark only after access approval and version reconciliation; use Phish360 only for clearly marked visual development experiments if the project owner accepts its research-use terms. Preserve all source labels, dates, exclusions, and license records. Do not derive independent-label status from feed membership, popularity rank, or screenshot appearance.
